Civ2RE v@VERSION@ - Civilization II Multiplayer Gold Edition for 64-bit Windows
https://github.com/Buyukcaglar/Civ2RE

You need your own copy of Civilization II Multiplayer Gold Edition; the game
is not included.

1. Put Civ2RE.exe in any folder and double-click it.
2. Show it your copy of the game: a zip, 7z or rar of it, its CD image
   (.cue/.bin or .iso), or a folder with the game (installed, or a copy of
   the CD).
3. It checks the game's files, copies the ones it needs into a GameData
   folder beside it, and starts the game. Afterwards it starts the game
   directly; keep Civ2RE.exe and GameData together.

Run "Civ2RE.exe --setup" to choose another copy.

Alt+Enter     full screen on or off (in full screen, the menu bar appears at
              the top edge of the screen)
Alt+F4        close (asks first during a game)
